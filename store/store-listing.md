# Slipper Rush: Play Store listing

## App name (30 characters max)

## Short description (80 characters max)
Dodge Mom's slipper, run on train roofs and race your own ghost!

## Full description (4000 characters max)
Mom found out you skipped your homework, and her slipper never misses! 🩴

Swipe, jump and roll through a colourful world tour. Dodge painted trucks and rickshaws, run up ramps onto train roofs, grab gold coins and escape before Mom catches you.

🌍 WORLD TOUR
Run through four places, each with its own look and obstacles:
• Old City Bazaar: painted trucks, fruit carts and washing lines
• Sunny Beach: sandcastles, palm trees and ice-cream trucks
• Mountain Road: pine trees, prayer flags and decorated buses
• Railway Station: trains, platforms and luggage trolleys

📖 STORY MODE
Five chapters with goals and boss chases. Escape Mom on her scooter, Dad on his motorbike and the Principal on his bicycle. Earn up to 3 stars in every chapter.

👻 GHOST RACE
Race a replay of your best run on exactly the same track, or send a challenge code to a friend on WhatsApp and see who is faster.

🐾 PET SIDEKICKS
Mithu the Parrot warns you early, Billo the Cat grabs coins, Moti the Puppy digs up treasure and Bakri the Goat headbutts obstacles out of your way.

🎨 STYLE YOUR RUNNER
Choose your face, hair, skin tone, clothes, shoes, hats and glasses. Pick a cartoon or realistic look.

⚡ POWER-UPS
Rocket Bag, Skateboard, Coin Magnet, Turbo Rush, Kite Flight, Double Score and Chai Time slow motion.

🏆 MORE TO DO
Trophies, missions, a daily gift, air tricks, monsoon storms and a day and night cycle.

No internet needed. No ads. Play anywhere!
