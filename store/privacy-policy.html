Slipper Rush: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Slipper Rush ("the game") is made by Asim (the developer). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

The short version

The game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. Everything you do in the game stays on your own phone.

What is saved on your phone

To remember your progress, the game saves the following only on your device: your coins, best scores, unlocked characters and items, the style you chose for your runner, story progress, completed missions, your settings (sound, music, vibration, graphics) and a replay of your best run for Ghost Race. This information never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Internet and third parties

The game does not need an internet connection and does not send any data to the developer or to third parties. It contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

If you choose to share a Ghost Race challenge code (for example with the Share or WhatsApp button), the code is passed to the app you pick. The code only contains your score and the path of your run, not your name or any personal information. What that app does with it is covered by its own privacy policy.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the game ever adds features that use personal information (for example ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
